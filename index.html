How the numbers
were called.

The source is SportsDataverse’s ESPN college football play-by-play release for the 2025 season. Each row represents one play record. The season file contains 166,053 rows across 956 games, 236 distinct possession/defense team names, and weeks 1–16. Source dates run from 2025-08-23 to 2026-01-20.

The raw file was reduced to 24 selected dashboard columns using scripts/prepare_data.py. No data rows were removed in preparation. The report-analysis script separates actual plays (`play == true`) from administrative rows; the 18,079 administrative rows remain in the published data file but are excluded from report rates and play-level averages. The resulting denominator is 147,974 actual plays. EPA and the relevant flags have no missing values in that subset. Rush/receiving yard fields are play-specific and naturally blank on other event types.

EPA averages are the arithmetic mean of `EPA` over included actual plays. Defensive EPA uses `def_EPA`, which is defined as the negative of offensive EPA: higher values mean more expected points denied. EPA success is the share with `EPA_success == true` (source definition: EPA > 0). Explosive rate is the share with `EPA_explosive == true`; this is the source’s EPA-based flag, not a custom yardage cutoff. Scoring rate is the share with `scoring_play == true`. Team offense and defense comparisons require at least 300 actual plays and are unadjusted for opponent strength. They group all actual-play records by the source’s possession/defense fields, including actual special-teams events; the retained columns do not include a separate scrimmage-play flag. Down, quarter, and week rates use the actual plays in that grouping; very small overtime and late-week samples are shown with counts.

Season type codes follow ESPN’s convention: 2 is regular season and 3 is postseason. Play-type comparisons are limited to the explicitly listed Rush/Rushing Touchdown and pass-coded labels shown in the figure; remaining event types are not silently assigned to either group. The release includes 25 plays with an unspecified play type and 564 missing wallclock values; neither field is needed to calculate the displayed EPA findings.
