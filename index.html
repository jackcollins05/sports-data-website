12THE FILM ROOM

How the season
was counted.

This report focuses on Football Bowl Subdivision teams. Team division is joined from the existing team branding metadata by ESPN team_id, not inferred from school names. FBS-only leaderboards exclude player and team-game rows classified as FCS or another division. FBS-versus-FCS games remain in the schedule and contribute the FBS team's score and statistics; the FCS opponent's player and team rows do not enter FBS leaderboards. The complete all-division prepared files remain available.

Player season totals come from the 2025 SportsDataverse espn_cfb_player_box dataset after category rows were pivoted to player-game rows and summed by player/team. Completion percentage, yards per attempt, yards per carry, yards per reception, and other rates are recalculated from season totals and attempts. Leaderboards apply the displayed workload thresholds. Passing entries affected by unresolved generic fields were all FCS quarterbacks; no affected FBS quarterback is included in the FBS pool.

Game dates, participants, scores, and winners come from espn_cfb_schedules. Traditional team performance comes from espn_cfb_team_box. One game-table row represents one scheduled game; one team-game row represents one FBS team's statistics in one game. FBS teams' matchups with FCS opponents remain included. Team scoring leaders require at least eight team-game rows; third-down comparisons require at least 80 attempts; player thresholds appear with each report finding.

The AP Top 25 tables contain the source's preseason poll, regular polls labeled Weeks 2–16, and its final poll. There is no regular poll labeled Week 1 in the source. In the report timeline, the preseason snapshot precedes regular-season results; poll “Week 2” is the poll published after schedule Week 1. AP ranks and movement are official source values; this project does not create its own rankings. An unranked team has a blank rank, never an invented No. 26.

Weekly scoring uses ESPN schedule season type 2 (regular season); postseason rows are excluded from that chart because postseason week numbers restart at 1. “Close game” means a final score margin of three points or fewer. No complete FBS-involving schedule games are excluded from the close-game count: all 934 have both scores. For other measures, only rows with a nonmissing value for that measure contribute; a rate is left unavailable when its denominator is missing or zero. Player season additive totals are initialized at zero and sum nonmissing game values, so a stored zero may mean no game-line value contributed or that recorded values summed to zero; the season file does not preserve a separate missingness flag for every additive statistic. Unresolved generic passing fields are preserved without guessing, and no FBS quarterback is affected.

Points per game and yards per game are the sum of nonmissing team-game values divided by the number of team-game rows with that value. Third- and fourth-down conversion percentages are total conversions divided by total attempts, multiplied by 100; completion percentage is completions divided by attempts, multiplied by 100. Passing yards per attempt, rushing yards per carry, and receiving yards per catch divide the corresponding season yard totals by attempts, carries, or receptions. Field-goal percentage is made kicks divided by attempts, multiplied by 100. Turnovers per game is the mean of nonmissing team-game turnover totals. Rates are recalculated from summed totals, not by averaging game-level rates, and a zero opportunity denominator produces no rate. Report leaderboards require 100 pass attempts for quarterbacks, 100 rush attempts for rushers, 40 receptions for receivers, eight games for defenders and team comparisons, and 80 third-down attempts in addition to eight team games for the third-down comparison. Dashboard player rate leaderboards use 100 pass attempts, 100 rush attempts, 40 receptions, or 10 field-goal attempts as appropriate; filtered team rates use the available filtered sample without an additional minimum.
