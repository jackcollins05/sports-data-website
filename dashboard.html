ANALYSIS

Season player leaders use the prepared player-season file. Completion and pass-efficiency rates require 100 attempts, yards per carry 100 rushes, yards per catch 40 receptions, and field-goal percentage 10 attempts; each rate is recomputed from totals. Selecting a week or season type loads player-game data on demand for weekly/game leaders. Poll periods preserve the published AP labels; “After Week 1” is the Week 2 poll released after schedule Week 1. Preseason and Final stay distinct.

DATA NOTE

Traditional totals come from the prepared ESPN/SportsDataverse box-score files. Rates use totals divided by reported opportunities. No AP ranks are estimated. The regular-season AP source has no Week 1 poll; preseason and final remain separately labeled. Game rank badges use the latest official AP poll published on or before each game date. Player-game statistics load only when a week or season type is selected.
